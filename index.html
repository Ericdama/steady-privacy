Steady — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Steady is a browser extension designed to help users block distracting websites and support focused browsing.

Information and local storage

Steady uses browser storage to save settings such as the websites and preferences a user chooses to block. These settings are used to provide the extension's features.

Website access and browsing activity

To apply blocking and redirect rules, the extension may evaluate website addresses against the user's configured rules. The extension is intended to perform this function within the browser and does not intentionally send browsing history or webpage content to the developer or an external server.

Sharing and selling data

Steady is not designed to sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising. If the extension's implementation or data practices change, this policy should be updated to reflect those changes.

Third-party services

This policy does not describe any third-party service unless one is added to the extension. If analytics, remote services, or other third-party integrations are introduced, this policy must be updated accordingly.

Security

Steady is designed to keep its settings in the browser. Users should protect access to their device and browser profile.

Children's privacy

Steady is a general-purpose browsing and productivity tool and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact the extension developer using the support contact listed on the Steady store listing.
